Our story

About D’Party

D’Party began with a simple belief: the sweetest moments deserve treats made with heart. What started as homemade pudding for family gatherings has grown into a beloved dessert service for celebrations all across the Atlanta area.

We proudly serve both our American and Hispanic neighbors with the same warmth, blending classic flavors, careful presentation and a personal touch that makes every event feel special.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve Atlanta, Georgia and the surrounding metro area, including nearby suburbs. Share your event location and we’ll confirm availability.

How far in advance should I place my order?

We recommend booking at least two to three weeks ahead for most events, and earlier for weddings, quinceañeras and large celebrations. We’ll always try to accommodate last-minute requests.

Do you provide treats for large events?

Yes. We cater intimate gatherings and large celebrations alike, scaling pudding cups, cotton candy and popcorn to your guest count.

Can I customize colors for my event?

Absolutely. We can match cotton candy and dessert-table styling to your event colors and theme.

Do you serve quinceañeras and weddings?

Yes. Quinceañeras, weddings, baby showers, corporate, school and church events are all part of what we do.

How can I request pricing?

Fill out our event inquiry form or text us at (678) 300-9112 and we’ll send a personalized quote.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes, we speak Spanish and Portuguese. Text us or fill out the form and we’ll gladly assist you.
